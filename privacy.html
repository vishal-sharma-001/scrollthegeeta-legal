Privacy Policy

Scroll The Geeta · last updated 2 November 2026

Scroll The Geeta is a Bhagavad Gita reading app. We collect as little as possible. We show no ads, and we use no advertising or third-party tracking tools.

What stays on your phone

If you never sign in, your notes, saved verses, reading progress, streak, language and settings are stored only on your device. We never see them. Downloaded content is stored encrypted on your device.

What reaches our server

  • An anonymous account by default. When you first like, comment or vote, the app creates an anonymous account on our server (Supabase). It has no name, email or phone number, only a random ID.
  • Optional email sign in. You may sign in with your email address. We use the address only to sign you in and to sync your data across devices. We do not send marketing email.
  • Sync, only when you are signed in. Your saved verses, notes, reading progress and plan are copied to our server so they follow you to a new phone. If you do not sign in, none of this leaves your device.
  • Likes, comments and votes. These are linked to your account ID. Comments are public and show the name you type. Before a comment is posted, its text is checked for abuse by OpenAI's moderation service, which does not use it to train models. You can delete your comments at any time.
  • Basic usage and crash data. The app sends a few anonymous events (for example, which screen was opened or that a verse was shared) and crash reports to our own server. They carry a random device ID, the app version and the event name, and never your name, email, notes or account ID. You can turn this off in Settings, under Share anonymous usage data.
  • Content downloads. Verses, paintings and audio are downloaded from our server. Like any web server, it briefly logs technical data such as IP address for security.

Payments

Gita Plus is sold through Google Play. Google handles payment and we never see your card or UPI details. We use RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active; it receives an anonymous app ID and your purchase record from Google Play.

Notifications

Daily verse and trial reminders are scheduled on your phone. No push server is involved.

Community rules

Comments are for thoughtful reflection on the Gita. No abuse, hate or spam. Use Report on any comment you find harmful, and Hide to stop seeing a person's comments. We may remove content or comments that break these rules.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices and deleting your data

You can delete your comments in the app, turn reminders off in Settings, or uninstall the app, which removes everything stored on your phone. To delete your account inside the app, open Settings and tap Delete my account (this works for anonymous accounts too, no sign in needed). This removes your synced data (saved verses, notes, progress and plan) and your likes, comments and votes from our server. It does not cancel a Google Play subscription; manage that in Google Play. You can also email scrollthegeeta@gmail.com with a deletion request and we will delete the data linked to your account.
