Privacy Policy

Scroll The Geeta · last updated 31 October 2026

Scroll The Geeta is a Bhagavad Gita reading app. We collect as little as possible. We show no ads and use no analytics or tracking tools.

What stays on your phone

Your notes, saved verses, reading progress, streak, language and settings are stored only on your device. We never see them. Downloaded content is stored encrypted on your device.

What reaches our server

  • An anonymous account. When you first like, comment or vote, the app creates an anonymous account on our server (Supabase). It has no name, email or phone number, only a random ID.
  • Likes, comments and votes. Likes and Seva votes are linked to that random ID. Comments are public and show the name you type. Before a comment is posted, its text is checked for abuse by OpenAI's moderation service, which does not use it to train models. You can delete your comments at any time.
  • Content downloads. Verses, paintings and audio are downloaded from our server. Like any web server, it briefly logs technical data such as IP address for security.

Payments

Gita Plus is sold through Google Play. Google handles payment and we never see your card or UPI details. We use RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active; it receives an anonymous app ID and your purchase record from Google Play.

Notifications

Daily verse and trial reminders are scheduled on your phone. No push server is involved.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

You can delete your comments in the app, turn reminders off in Settings, or uninstall the app, which removes everything stored on your phone. To delete your anonymous account and its likes and votes, email us.
