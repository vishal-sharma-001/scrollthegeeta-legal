Delete your account

Scroll The Geeta · last updated 6 November 2026

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Scroll The Geeta.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

This works for anonymous accounts too, no sign in needed.

By email

If you no longer have the app, write to scrollthegeeta@gmail.com from the email you signed in with (or include your account ID from Settings) with the subject "Delete my account". We delete the data within 30 days and confirm by reply.

What is deleted

  • Your account and its ID
  • Synced saved verses, notes, reading progress and plan
  • Your likes, comments and seva votes

What is kept

  • Anonymous usage and crash events, which carry no account ID, are kept for up to 12 months.
  • Purchase records held by Google Play and RevenueCat, which tax and accounting law require, for as long as required.

Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel it in Google Play, Payments and subscriptions, Subscriptions.
