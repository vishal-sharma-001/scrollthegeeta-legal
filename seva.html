Seva Policy

Scroll The Geeta · last updated 6 November 2026

Seva is selfless service. We set aside part of Gita Plus revenue for causes that feed, heal and teach, and subscribers decide where it goes.

How much

10% of what we receive from Google Play for every Gita Plus purchase, that is, the amount left after Google Play's service fee and the taxes Google collects. Refunded purchases are not counted.

Who decides

Each month, every active Gita Plus subscriber can cast one vote in the Seva tab for one of the listed causes, and can change it until the month ends. At the end of the month the seva share is split between the causes in proportion to the votes. If no votes are cast, the share is split equally.

The causes

  • Shri Mataji Gaushala: shelter and free care for rescued, old and sick cows in Barsana, Braj (gau seva). matajigaushala.org
  • Sankara Eye Foundation: free eye surgery for people who cannot pay. sankaraeye.com
  • Ekal Vidyalaya Foundation: one-teacher schools in remote villages. ekal.org

These organisations are independent and have not endorsed this app. We may change the list; changes are announced in the app before a new month starts.

When it is sent

Google Play pays developers about a month after each sale. We send the seva share within 60 days of receiving it, directly to each organisation through its official donation channel. Very small amounts may be carried over until they reach a sensible transfer size.

Reporting

The "Given so far" total in the Seva tab is updated only after a transfer has actually been made. We keep the donation receipts and will share a summary on request.

Tax

Gita Plus is a purchase of app features. You are not making a donation, and no tax receipt is issued to you.
