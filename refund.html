Refund Policy

Scroll The Geeta · last updated 6 November 2026

Who handles payments

Gita Plus is sold through Google Play. Google processes every payment, so refunds follow Google Play's refund rules and are requested from Google, not from us.

Free trial

If your plan includes a 7-day free trial, you are not charged during the trial. Cancel at least a day before it ends in Google Play, Subscriptions, and you will not be charged at all. The app can remind you before the trial ends.

Asking for a refund

Within 48 hours of a purchase you can request a refund yourself at play.google.com, Account, Order history, then Request a refund. After 48 hours, write to us at scrollthegeeta@gmail.com with your Google Play order number and we will help you ask Google, who makes the final decision.

Cancelling

Cancelling stops future renewals. You keep Gita Plus until the end of the period you have already paid for. Cancelling does not refund the current period.

Lifetime purchase

The one-time lifetime purchase follows the same 48-hour self-service window. After that, contact us and we will look at each request fairly.

Seva

A share of every subscription is set aside for seva. If you receive a refund, that share is returned with it.
